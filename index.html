Tiny glossary

Words you will meet in the lab.

Photon
A quantum of light—the smallest amount of light energy that can be emitted or absorbed in an interaction.
Wavefunction (ψ)
A mathematical description of a quantum system that lets us calculate the probabilities of different measurement results.
Probability
A number that tells us how likely a particular measurement result is.
Measurement
An interaction between a quantum system and its surroundings that produces a definite recorded result.
Qubit
A quantum system that can be used to represent information, with measurement outcomes associated with 0 and 1.
Entanglement
A quantum relationship in which the state of one system is correlated with the state of another in ways that cannot be described as independent systems.

This is not just theory

Quantum ideas are already part of your world.

Solar panels

Use the photoelectric effect to convert energy from light into electrical energy.

LEDs and neon signs

Rely on electrons changing energy states and releasing light with particular energies.

Electron microscopes

Use electrons and their wave properties to reveal details far smaller than what visible light microscopes can resolve.

Quantum computers

Use quantum states, measurement, and phenomena such as superposition and entanglement to process information in ways that differ from classical computers.
